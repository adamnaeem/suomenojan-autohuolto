Repair

Diagnostics to engine work, all makes

Something rattles, leaks or refuses to start? Bring it in. We diagnose first, explain what we found in plain language, then repair what actually needs repairing.

  • R-01

    Fault diagnostics

    OBD readout and hands-on troubleshooting for warning lights, electrical faults and strange noises. Diagnostic fee from €55, waived if we carry out the repair.

  • R-02

    Brakes and suspension

    Pads, discs, calipers, shock absorbers and control arms. Brake pad replacement per axle from €120 incl. parts.

  • R-03

    From gasket leaks to clutch and gearbox work. Larger jobs always quoted in writing with parts and labour itemised.

    Exhaust and emissions

    Exhaust repair and replacement, lambda sensors, DPF and catalyst issues. We help you through the inspection (katsastus) if emissions are the problem.

Tires

Seasonal changes, storage and repair

Two seasons, one visit each

Finnish weather is not negotiable. We swap your summer and winter tires, check tread depth and pressure, and torque the bolts to spec. Store the off-season set with us and skip hauling heavy wheels up the stairwell.

Nail in the tread? Most punctures can be repaired from the inside for a fraction of a new tire. Sidewall damage means replacement, and we will tell you straight which one it is.
